Reading fellow data

What a fellow sees, and what only staff see

A fellow sees

Their own attendance calendar and arrival times

Their scores and the feedback an instructor approved

Their goals, and the instructors’ comments on them

The answers a coach marked as shared

Only staff see

Performance groups and colors

Staff-only coaching answers and notes on a fellow

Themes, the AI summary of a fellow’s record

Which grading group, called a cohort, a fellow is in

A fellow may ask to read their record, so staff write notes as they would say them to the fellow.

If someone asks what the LMS stores about a fellow: name, email, GitHub username, attendance, submitted work, grades, goals and coaching records. It stores no date of birth, address, phone number, government identifier or payment detail.
