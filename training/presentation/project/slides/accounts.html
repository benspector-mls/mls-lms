How it is organized

Three kinds of account

Fellows

Check in each morning, read lessons, hand in work, read feedback, and keep their own goals.

Instructors

Write assignments, take attendance, grade, follow each fellow’s performance, and run coaching sessions.

Admins

Do everything an instructor does in every program, and decide who else is staff.

Everybody signs in with a GitHub account. The LMS has no read-only staff account today, so a figure about a fellow comes to you from the programs team.

This is where to say who to ask. Name the person on the programs team that each department should go to for a figure about a fellow. The lack of a read-only account is a good candidate for the feedback at the end.
