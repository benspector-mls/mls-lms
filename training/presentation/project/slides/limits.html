AI and limits

What the LMS does not do yet

Salesforce

Grades are still re-entered by hand. The connection awaits setup on the Salesforce side.

Trends across a roster

Each fellow has a trends page. A reading of the whole roster over time is not built.

Progress by skill

Scores are per assignment. Scores grouped by skill are in development.

Email

The LMS sends none. Its only notifications are Slack messages that each person turns on.

Knowing these limits tells you which questions about fellows still need a person on the programs team to answer.

Keep this to one minute. These four are the limits most likely to matter to people outside the programs team. This slide leads into the feedback: a limit that affects their work is exactly what to raise next.
