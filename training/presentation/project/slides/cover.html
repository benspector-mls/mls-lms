The Marcy LMS

What it records about a fellow, how to read it, and what it should do next for your work.

Before the talk: open two browser windows. Window 1 is the live LMS on the test program, signed in as an admin. Window 2 is the development copy on the Test Program, open at Fellows, Performance tab. Opening line: most of you have never signed in to the LMS, and you do not need an account to follow this. By the end you will be able to describe what it records, read a fellow's Performance row, and tell me what you need from it.
