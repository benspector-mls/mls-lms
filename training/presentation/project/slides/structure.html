A fellow joins a program once and is then a student of every course in it. Attendance is taken once a day for the whole program.

Two words to define out loud. A program is one running of everything a group of fellows does together. A course is one subject inside it. Mention that "cohort" in the LMS means how a roster is divided between instructors for grading, and that fellows never see cohorts.
