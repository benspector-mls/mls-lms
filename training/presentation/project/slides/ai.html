AI and limits

Where AI is used, and who checks it

FeatureWhat the AI writesWho checks it
Grading reportsA draft score and feedback for each section of an assignmentAn instructor edits and approves it before the fellow sees anything
Checks for understandingA level and two sentences of feedback on a fellow’s short answerThe fellow sees it at once. It is never graded, and an instructor can override it
ThemesThree to six themes from a fellow’s record, before a coaching sessionStaff only. The coach corrects it, and the fellow never sees it
TutorAnswers to a fellow’s questions, drawn from the course lessonsStaff can read every conversation. It will not write assignment code

Before coaching notes are sent to the AI for Themes, the LMS replaces names and a list of sensitive terms.

The AI model is Claude, made by Anthropic. If asked about cost or about data retention, offer to follow up after the talk and do not guess.
