Reading fellow data

One color rule sorts every fellow into a group

Red: under 75 percent

Yellow: under 90 percent

Green: 90 percent and over

No color: fewer than 4 items counted

Needs support

Meeting the bar

Exceeding expectations

Every colored cell is green

Too early to say
