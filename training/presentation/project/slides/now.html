Why it exists

Now an instructor approves once

The LMS drafts the grading report. The instructor reads it, corrects it, and approves it. That one approval does four things:

Records the grade

Marks the work complete or incomplete

Posts the feedback on the fellow’s code

Shows the feedback to the fellow

Salesforce is the one step still done by hand. The LMS side of that connection is built, and the Salesforce side awaits setup.

Stress that nothing reaches a fellow until a person approves it. That question comes up every time AI grading is mentioned, so answer it here before anyone asks.
