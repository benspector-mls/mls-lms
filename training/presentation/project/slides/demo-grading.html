A course, Triage, then the grading queue

Triage answers one question: what is waiting on me. Fellows’ questions come first.

The LMS drafts a report against the instructor’s rubric. The instructor edits the scores and the wording.

Work that earns 75 percent of its points counts as complete, unless the instructor sets a different threshold.

About 4 minutes. Leave View as. Open the course's Triage, press Grade all ungraded submissions, and open a submission whose report was generated before the talk, because generating one takes too long to watch. Edit one score, then press Release Feedback. Use View as again to show the feedback on the fellow's side. Then show the Gradebook for 20 seconds.
