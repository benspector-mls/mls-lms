Teaching and grading

Marcy LMS quick reference · page 1 of 2

TaskWhereRemember
Take the morning check-inAttendance › TodayOne four-digit code a day covers every course. A program with a schedule opens check-in two hours before class without anyone pressing a button.
Correct one fellow’s attendanceAttendance › The whole term › a dateEach fellow has Present, Late, Absent and Excused. The grid shows the last two weeks until you change it.
Remove a holiday or a breakAttendance › Schedule › Remove daysA break nobody removes marks every fellow absent. Removing the days afterwards removes those absences.
Write an assignmentCourse › Coursework › Add assignment, inside a unitThe kind is fixed once the assignment exists. Individual or team hand-in is fixed once it is published.
Set what counts as completeAssignment form › How it is graded75 percent of the points unless you change it. Changing it re-marks every submission already graded.
Publish an assignmentCourse › CourseworkFellows cannot see an unpublished assignment at all.
Write or change a rubricRubrics, then the Rubric field of a sectionSaving a change makes a new version. Only an admin can change the program’s own rubrics.
See what is waiting on youCourse › TriageFellows’ unread comments come first, because a fellow with a question is stopped until somebody answers.
Grade everything that is waitingTriage › Grade all ungraded submissionsOne queue across every assignment in the course. It holds only your cohort when you have chosen one.
Draft, edit and release a reportGrading queue › Generate report › Release FeedbackA report is a draft until you approve it. Approving shows the feedback to the fellow at once; no second step exists.
Grade by handGrading queue › Write it yourselfEvery section needs a score and feedback. The LMS refuses to release an empty section as a zero.
Answer a fellow’s questionGrading queue › Comments tabA dot on the tab means a reply is owed. Mark resolved on Triage clears a question answered in person.
Grade a resubmissionTriage › ResubmissionsIt is graded fresh. Previous feedback lists what the fellow was told in earlier rounds.
Read or export the gradebookCourse › Gradebook › Overview, Download CSVOverview is one row per fellow: completed, late, missing, and what is waiting on you.
Split the grading with a colleagueProgram Settings › Cohorts, then the cohort pickerA cohort is a filter and grants nothing. Any instructor of the program can still grade any fellow.
Get Slack messages about your gradingYour name › ProfileBoth settings are off until you turn them on. The LMS sends no email.

Following and coaching fellows

The worst cell on a row decides the group: any red is Needs support; yellow with no red is Meeting the bar; all green is Exceeding expectations; nothing due yet, or fewer than 5 attendance sessions, is Too early to say.

TaskWhereRemember
Find who needs supportFellows › PerformanceHover a cell to read its rule. Fellows never see this tab, their group, or the colors.
Change the weeks being readFellows › Performance › the dates above the tableRecent columns read the last two weeks until you change the dates. Term columns always read the whole term.
Ask about one measure or one courseFellows › Performance › measure and course filtersThe groups are then decided by the chosen measures and courses alone.
Open one fellow’s recordFellows › Roster › the fellow’s nameFour tabs: Performance, Coaching, Trends and Tutor. Each tab has its own address, so a link can name one.
Start a coaching sessionFellow’s record › Coaching › Start coaching sessionIt is a draft the fellow cannot see, so you can start it days early. Set the session date to the day you meet.
Complete a coaching sessionThe session form › Complete sessionThe fellow then sees the dated figures and every answer labeled as shared with them. The form closes to editing.
Throw away a session started by mistakeThe session form › DiscardDiscard is offered on drafts only. A completed session stays on the record.
Comment on a fellow’s goalFellow’s record › Coaching › Goals › a goalGoals belong to the fellow and you cannot edit one. A comment cannot be edited, only withdrawn.
Write a note about a fellowFellow’s record › Coaching › NotesFellows never see notes, but a fellow may ask to read their record. Write what you would say to them.
Prepare with ThemesFellow’s record › Trends › Themes › GenerateStaff only. Correct a wrong theme before relying on it, because the next summary builds on your version.
Compare fellows’ latest sessionsFellows › CoachingThe table reads one coaching form at a time. Choose the form above the table.
Export a fellow’s coaching historyFellow’s record › Coaching › Sessions › Download CSVThe file holds the staff-only answers, so handle it as you would the record itself.
Read a check for understandingCourse › Coursework › the check’s rowA fellow gets up to three attempts and is never graded on one. You can set a different level on any attempt.
Read what a fellow asked the TutorFellow’s record › TutorEvery conversation across the program’s courses is listed.
Correct a fellow’s nameFellow’s record › the pencil beside the nameThe new name replaces the old one in every course and on the fellow’s own screen.
