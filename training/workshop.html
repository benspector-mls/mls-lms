Marcy LMS Workshop

Sixty minutes for the programs team to practice the parts of the LMS each person has not used yet. Keep the guide and the quick reference open beside this page.

By the end you will be able to

  • Grade one submission from the all-ungraded queue by drafting or writing, editing, and releasing a report.
  • Record and complete a coaching session for a test student, and identify which answers the fellow will see.
  • Given the Performance tab of your own program, explain why each of three fellows sits in its group, naming the cell that decided it.
  • Use the quick reference to locate the screen for a task you have never performed.

Where you work

Exercises A, B and D change records, so you perform them in the Workshop test program, on the test student assigned to you. A test program is kept out of Salesforce, and a test student is left out of every attendance and performance figure, so nothing you do there touches a real fellow.

Exercise C only reads figures, and a test program has none to read, so you perform it in your own real program. Exercise C asks you to change nothing.

Write only invented details about a test student. Generating Themes in Exercise B sends the session answers and notes you typed to the AI model.

Exercise A: grade a submission and answer a question

Workshop test program · 15 minutes

Goal

Your test student has handed in a link and asked a question about the assignment. Answer the question, then release a grade and feedback to them.

Work it out in this order

  1. Open the Workshop course. Which screen opens first, and what is at the top of it?
  2. Find your test student’s question and reply to it where they will read it.
  3. Open the queue that holds every ungraded submission in the course, and find your test student.
  4. Produce a report: press Generate report, or press Write it yourself and fill in each section.
  5. Change one score and one sentence, then release the report.

Check your result

  • Your test student has left the To do tab and appears on the Graded tab.
  • Triage no longer lists their question under Unread comments.
  • The gradebook shows a score in their cell. Is the work complete or incomplete, and which setting on the assignment decided that?

If you cannot proceed

Read Triage: what is waiting on you and Grading in the guide.

Exercise B: record a coaching session

Workshop test program · 15 minutes

Goal

Hold an invented coaching conversation with your test student and leave their record as you would after a real one.

Work it out in this order

  1. Open your test student’s record from the roster and find where coaching lives.
  2. Start a coaching session and set the session date to today.
  3. Before typing, count the questions labeled Shared with the fellow’s name on completion. Every other question is labeled Staff only. Then answer at least three questions, including one shared and one staff-only.
  4. Complete the session. Read the confirmation before you accept it.
  5. Write one note about the conversation in the Notes section.
  6. Open the Trends tab and generate Themes. Edit one theme that you think is wrong or thin.

Check your result

  • The confirmation named how many answers the fellow will see. Did that number match the shared answers you filled in?
  • The session appears in the Sessions list with today’s date and the name of the form, and it can no longer be edited.
  • Themes lists at least one theme, and your edit is saved. The themes will be short, because a test student’s record holds very little.

If you cannot proceed

Read Coaching a fellow in the guide.

Exercise C: explain three fellows’ groups

Your real program · read only · 12 minutes

Goal

Explain to the person beside you why three of your fellows sit in the groups they do.

Work it out in this order

  1. Open the Performance tab of the Fellows screen in your own program.
  2. Choose one fellow from each of three different groups. For each one, name the single cell that decided their group and say its rule. Hover the cell to check yourself.
  3. Find one cell with no color. What would have to be true before the LMS colors it?
  4. Change the dates above the table to the last four weeks. Did any of your three fellows change group, and which cell moved them?
  5. Open the Coaching tab and find the same three fellows. How long ago was each one’s latest session?

Check your result

  • Your partner can repeat, for each fellow, which cell decided the group.
  • You changed nothing: dates and filters are choices about what the page shows, and they are kept in the page’s address.

If you cannot proceed

Read Performance: who needs support in the guide, or press How does bucketing work? above the table.

Preparation for the facilitator, before the session

Each step is performed by an admin on the live LMS. Allow about an hour. Steps 6 and 7 are repeated once per attendee, because two people cannot release the same report and each person needs a question of their own to answer.

During the session, show the fellow’s side once on the shared screen after Exercise A, using View as, because only an admin can view as a test student. After the session, archive the Workshop program.
